About

I work at the intersection of statistics and biology, currently as a Principal Research Associate at Moderna, where I focus on turning complex data into translational decisions that scientists can act on.

My work spans non-clinical, chemistry and delivery science data, along with in vitro and in vivo studies. Alongside the analysis itself, I build reproducible pipelines and internal tools that turn repetitive, manual analyses into something scientists can run in a fraction of the time.

Right now, I'm also expanding into the omics space, a newer area for me that I'm excited to grow into.

From the doctor's side

Biological grounding: thinking in mechanisms, disease, and what actually happens in a cell or a patient.

From the computer scientist's side

Statistical and computational rigor: building models and pipelines that hold up under scrutiny.
